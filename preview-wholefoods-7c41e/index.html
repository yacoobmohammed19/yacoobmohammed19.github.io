Good for everyday wellbeing

Wholesome foods can be part of a balanced diet. Here is why customers love our Greek-style yoghurt range.

💪

A source of protein

Our full-cream Greek-style yoghurt lists 4g of protein per 100g on the label, and the strained style is higher at 10g per 100g.

🦴

Contains calcium

Dairy is a well-known source of calcium, which is part of a normal, balanced diet. The label shows 120mg per 100g.

Active cultures

Made with live, active yoghurt cultures, the traditional way yoghurt has been enjoyed for generations.

Satisfying & versatile

Rich and creamy. Enjoy it with fruit, honey and nuts, in dips and dressings, or as a cooling side with spicy meals.
